# Mind mapping workshop

I attended a mind mapping workshop on 16/07/26 at the office.  
After learning it, I thought, why didn't I know this before? It is such a really useful tool for generating ideas for final work, like writing an article or giving a presentation.  
You can use it for writing notes as well. In the session, they mainly demonstrated its use for idea generation. The session started with writing down a page about the project you are currently working on. Then they taught the idea of mind mapping. We drew mind maps on different topics. Then I realized that even if I am not an expert in a particular subject, I can still generate a few ideas and talk about that subject for 4-5 minutes or write a paragraph about it.  
Then, at the end of the session, we drew a mind map on an existing project that we are working on.  
### End of session I realized that:
- I was able to generate more ideas
- Came up with better and related ideas
- No need to think about structure - what will come first, then what will come next. Just write down the ideas as they come.  
### So in my daily life, I use mind maps for 2 things:
- Generating ideas about a subject
- Taking notes on a subject
